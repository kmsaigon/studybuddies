About StudyBuddies

StudyBuddies helps university students connect with peers in the same courses to form study groups. Find study partners based on your university, courses, location, availability, and study preferences.

Map-based discovery, course matching, and simple group management make it easy to find study groups that fit your schedule and learning style.
